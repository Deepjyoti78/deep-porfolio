## Overview

**Detour** is an editorial self-drive road trip portal designed for travellers seeking adventure off the beaten track. Crafted with warm nostalgic visual tones and bold Americana-inspired typography, the platform invites users to step away from the daily routine and embark on curated signature scenic drives.

From river bluffs and waterfalls to misty mountain passes and coastal highways, Detour provides detailed route schematics, collection depot checklists, and an intuitive fleet reservation engine.

## Key Features

- **Curated Signature Routes**: Interactive journey guides featuring detailed elevation profiles, scenic waypoint markers, and local road condition advisories.
- **Schematic Route Maps**: High-fidelity visual route schematics highlighting scenic stops, rest areas, and viewpoint coordinates.
- **National Collection Depots**: Multi-hub pickup and return network across major Indian metropolitan centers (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Goa, Jaipur) with quick airport access.
- **Interactive Fleet Discovery**: Date and location interval filter allowing drivers to reserve vehicles suited for mountain ghats or highway cruising.
- **Station Guides & Checklists**: Downloadable vehicle handover checklists and departure preparation guides for drivers.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Next.js, Tailwind CSS
- **Design System**: Warm vintage editorial typography with custom SVG schematics
- **Deployment**: Vercel Cloud Platform
