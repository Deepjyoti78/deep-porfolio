## Overview

**Lunelle** is an atmospheric, editorial romantic discovery platform crafted for two. Conceived as an ethereal digital sanctuary rather than a generic utility, Lunelle combines breathtaking planetary vistas, warm sunrise illumination, and tactile frosted glass surfaces to help couples plan meaningful dates, unearth secluded destinations, preserve private milestones, and cultivate shared rituals.

The platform marries minimalist, restrained typography with thoughtful micro-interactions, responsive touch geometry, and ambient soundscapes synthesized via the Web Audio API—creating a cinematic visual identity that feels like entering a private universe tailored for two.

## Key Features

- **Cinematic Earth Horizon Hero**: Full-bleed hero capturing a radiant amber sunrise, curved Earth horizon, starry celestial heavens, and a silhouette couple atop a scenic promontory with custom radial gradient scrims.
- **Frosted Glass Navigation Deck**: Floating left-shifted frosted glass capsule (`backdrop-blur-md`) housing interactive routes for **Discover**, **Date Ideas**, **Places**, and **Stories**, flanked by the custom Lunelle wordmark and a rounded *"Our Space ↗"* portal.
- **Curated Date Discovery Engine**: Interactive date exploration deck featuring curated experiences categorized by mood, budget, and season—from starlight stargazing picnics to midnight acoustic jazz cellars.
- **Sanctuary Atlas (Places to Explore)**: Curated collection of secluded, scenic getaways across continents with coordinates, seasonal tips, and intimate travel notes.
- **Real Couple Chronicles (Stories)**: Warm editorial reader sharing genuine stories and reflective essays celebrating connection, exploration, and quiet companionship.
- **Our Space (Private Orbit for Two)**: An intimate shared portal featuring synchronous anniversary countdowns, collaborative bucket lists, shared memory vaults, and joint dream itineraries.
- **Audio Atmosphere Synthesis**: Native Web Audio synthesizer generating subtle harmonic ambient textures that breathe life into the cosmic visual backdrop upon interaction.
- **Full Mobile Responsiveness**: Precision-crafted responsive hierarchy featuring smooth horizontal scroll carousels for bottom feature chips, fluid typography scaling, and touch-optimized bottom sheets on mobile viewports.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React
- **Audio Architecture**: Web Audio API (`AudioContext`, custom dual-oscillator ambient sound synthesis)
- **Typography**: Inter Sans & Instrument Serif Italic typography pairings
- **Design System**: Warm charcoal-and-cream palette (`#100D0B`, `#251710`, `#D88B55`, `#F5E5D2`) with frosted glassmorphic pill components
- **Build & Performance**: Vite, PostCSS, ESLint
