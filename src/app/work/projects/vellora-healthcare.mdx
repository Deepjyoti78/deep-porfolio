## Overview

**Vellora Healthcare** is a modern clinical care platform designed to make compassionate, specialized medical attention accessible directly at the patient's doorstep. Combining digital scheduling intelligence with certified in-home doctor visits, Vellora bridges the gap between traditional clinical infrastructure and contemporary home healthcare needs.

Trusted by over 135,000 patients and backed by 30+ board-certified medical specialists across 18 clinical departments, the platform provides unhurried bedside examinations, preventative family medicine, and continuous chronic condition monitoring through a high-performance web experience.

## Key Features

- **Doorstep Specialist Consultations**: On-demand booking engine connecting families with licensed physicians for comprehensive, unhurried bedside medical examinations.
- **Categorized Specialists Directory**: Instant faceted filtering across 48+ verified clinicians spanning General & Acute Care, Internal Medicine & Geriatrics, Pediatrics, and Orthopedic Rehabilitation.
- **Same-Day Appointment Availability**: Real-time physician calendar synchronization providing same-day clinical visits and emergency home triage slots.
- **Multi-Role Portal Access**: Integrated session switching supporting tailored dashboards for Guests, registered Patients, and practicing Clinicians.
- **Patient Health Monitoring**: Centralized management for recurring checkups, chronic condition tracking (hypertension, diabetes, cardiology), and digital visit summaries.
- **Transparent Doctor Profiles**: Detailed clinician portfolios highlighting credentials, board certifications, years of hospital practice, verified patient ratings (4.9/5 avg), and treatment philosophies.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React 19, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React
- **Architecture**: Component-driven UI with responsive layout grids and optimized media loading
- **Deployment**: Vercel Cloud Platform with continuous integration
